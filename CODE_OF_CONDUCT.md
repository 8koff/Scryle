# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place to work, for everyone.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and helpful.
- Welcome questions, especially from people new to the project.
- Respect people's time, privacy and differing views.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussion.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers (a direct message to a maintainer on GitHub). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and may block people who break these rules.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
